      FAQ

      Good questions, straight answers

      Is it really free?

      Yes — free forever, no sign-up, no ads. Translation runs through the free MyMemory API, and your saved listings live in your own browser, not on a server.

      Do I have to install anything?

      No. It's a normal website that works in any browser. If you use it often you can tap Install app (or “Add to Home Screen” on iPhone) and it opens like a native app.

      How accurate is the translation?

      It's machine translation, so read the English with a grain of salt. The important part: rent, deposit and phone numbers are extracted from the original Thai digits, so those numbers are always exact.

      Can it read screenshots of a post?

      Not yet — OCR isn't wired into this build. Copy the post's text instead (it takes a second), and attach the screenshot to the photo tray so you keep it with the listing.

      What about FR-5, translate-selected-text?

      Translating text you highlight on other webpages needs browser privileges a plain website can't have, so that feature lives in the Chrome extension only.

      Where are my saved listings stored?

      In your browser's local storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Export to CSV if you want a backup or want to share your shortlist with a friend.
